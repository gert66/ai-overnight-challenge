One night, five working programs — and it started with a walk

After our physics meeting about AI, Gert wondered how he could show colleagues what AI can do today, more than just prompt in, answer out. During a walk he talked it through with ChatGPT via Voice Mode on his phone — without touching a keyboard. In about half an hour, the concept, five very different software challenges and the first setup took shape.
