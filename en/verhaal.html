The story, in plain language

After a meeting with physics colleagues about
AIAI (artificial intelligence) is software that understands language, can reason, and can create text, plans or code itself — a kind of very fast, versatile assistant.
Gert kept thinking about it: how do you show colleagues what AI can do, beyond asking a question and getting an answer?
During a walk, he started talking out loud to ChatGPT via
Voice ModeVoice Mode is a feature that lets you talk out loud to an AI app and get a spoken answer back — no typing needed.
. No keyboard: just a conversation on the go. In about half an hour, the concept, five software challenges and the first setup took shape.
AI then expanded the rough ideas itself into full assignments with
acceptance criteriaAcceptance criteria are the concrete, measurable requirements a solution must meet before it counts as ‘done’.
: what exactly did each program need to be able to do?
Then an
autonomousAutonomous here means: working independently, without anyone needing to keep intervening or approving.
environment got to work — for a whole night. The system planned, built, tested, had its work independently checked, repaired it, and recorded every step. No one needed to step in.
The next morning, five working
browser programsBrowser programs run directly in your web browser — no installation needed, just open a link.
were ready. Exactly what Gert wanted to show.
