One night, five working programs, and it started with a walk

After a meeting about AI, Gert wondered how he could show colleagues what AI can do today, more than just asking a question and getting an answer. The result: five different, genuinely working programs that you can open for yourself below.

How it went

  1. The meeting
    After a physics meeting about AI, the question came up: what can AI really do?
  2. The walk
    During a walk, Gert started bouncing ideas off AI, just by talking.
  3. About 30 minutes, no keyboard
    It all happened through Voice Mode on his phone. No laptop, no keyboard touched.
  4. Five ideas
    Together they arrived at five very different small software challenges.
  5. AI expands the assignments
    AI expanded the rough ideas itself into full assignments, acceptance criteria and executable instructions.
  6. Working autonomously overnight
